# Metrics

Every schedule is judged on the same numbers, as median, 90th percentile, 99th percentile and
maximum. Minutes only matter at the places where a viewer waits; inside a show use ratios.

## Inside a show

- **Mid-roll / content**: mid-break time divided by the show's own length.
- **Pre + mid + post / content**: the whole ad load around a show.

## Where a viewer waits (minutes)

- **End of a slot**: post + tail + any dead air after the last program of a window.
- **Between two programs of a window**: post (+ the next pre) + dead air.
- **Beginning of a slot**: head + pre.
- Also: the number of window ends with more than 10 minutes, and more than 30 minutes.

## Whole lineup

- **Grid accuracy**: share of programs starting within 1 second of a grid line, and the worst miss.
  Target: 100%, worst under a second.
- **Ad time** as a share of air time, and how it splits between mid, post and tail.
- **Dead air** as a share of air time, and as gaps per day by size.
- **Blocks with no ads**, and shows of 25 minutes or more with no mid break.
- **Longest run of ads in a row.**
- **Repeats**: how often an ordered show's cycle repeats.

## Rules for measuring

- Leave out the day of a daylight-saving change.
- A window's boundaries come from the schedule's clock, not from the viewer's.
- Count what is written to the lineup, not what the guide shows (the guide hides ad filler).
